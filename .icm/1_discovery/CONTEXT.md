# Stage 1: Discovery (`1_discovery`)

## Stage Scope
This directory holds discovery artifacts, theme audits, and research notes for `ron-hajewski-shopify-portfolio`.

## Responsibilities & Inputs
- **Theme Structure Auditing:** Inspect existing files in `sections/`, `snippets/`, `templates/`, `layout/`, `assets/`, and `config/` to establish ground truth before planning or editing.
- **Schema & Settings Inventory:** Document existing section schemas, color schemes in `config/settings_data.json` and `config/settings_schema.json`, and base templates (such as `templates/product.json`).
- **Storefront & Resource Mapping:** Record real product handles, collection structures, and metafield definitions. Never fabricate GIDs, handles, or schema blocks.

## Expected Outputs
- Audit summaries, existing schema snapshots, and feasibility notes that feed directly into `2_planning/`.
